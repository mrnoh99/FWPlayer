The DST decoder in this directory (ccp_calc, dst_ac, dst_data, dst_fram,
dst_init, unpack_dst, types.h, conststr.h) is taken unmodified from SACD Ripper:

    https://github.com/sacd-ripper/sacd-ripper  (libs/libdstdec)

It is licensed under the GNU General Public License, version 2 (see the headers
of each file) and derives from the ISO/IEC 14496-3 reference decoder. FWPlayer is
a personal-use app that is not distributed, so the GPL's conditions on
distribution don't apply; if you ever distribute FWPlayer, they do.

fwdst.c / include/fwdst.h are a small single-threaded wrapper written for FWPlayer.
